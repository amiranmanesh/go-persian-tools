# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith. Argue about the code,
not the person writing it.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the change, with a reason. "This breaks callers who store the
  output" is useful; "this is wrong" is not.
- Accept that people have different levels of experience with Go, with Persian
  orthography, and with Unicode. Explaining is part of the work.
- Write in whichever of English or Persian you are comfortable with. Nobody is
  turned away for their grammar in either.
- Take a correction as information, not as an attack.

## What is not

- Insults, personal attacks, or dismissing someone because of who they are.
- Harassment of any kind, in the repository or in private.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion, or repeatedly reopening a decision that
  has been made and explained.
- Publishing someone's private information.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/amiranmanesh/go-persian-tools/security/advisories/new),
which is the private channel this repository has. Reports stay between you and
the maintainers.

Maintainers will respond in proportion to what happened: usually a private word,
sometimes editing or removing a comment, and for repeated or serious behavior, a
block from the repository. Maintainers who do not hold themselves to this
document should be held to it by the others.

## Scope

This applies to everything in this repository — issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages and code — and to private contact that starts
here.
